# 06-AI: Autonomous Bot Fleet & Intelligent Modules

## AI Integration Architecture
Setupss embeds AI natively across the entire platform rather than as a standalone chatbot.

---

## Core AI Modules
1. **AI Builder & Compatibility Engine**: Validates PSU wattage, physical case clearance, motherboard socket compatibility, and RAM height clearance in real-time.
2. **Bottleneck Predictor**: Calculates exact CPU vs GPU bottlenecks across 1080p, 1440p, and 4K resolutions.
3. **Autonomous 30-Bot Fleet**: 
   - 30 distinct background sentinel bots (Validator-X, Sentinel-Prime, Link-Check, Security-Scan, Performance-Bot, etc.) running continuous 7/24 audits on SEO, database integrity, security, telemetry, and frontend responsiveness.
4. **AI Assistant & Content Advisor**: Real-time chat guidance powered by Google Gemini server-side SDK.
