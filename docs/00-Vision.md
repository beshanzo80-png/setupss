# 00-Vision: Setupss Technical & Product Vision

## Core Philosophy: "The Operating System of PC Enthusiasts"
Setupss is **not** just a setup sharing website. It is the definitive operating system and social ecosystem for PC enthusiasts worldwide. 

### Why We Exist
Traditional platforms are fragmented:
- **PCPartPicker** has hardware compatibility, but no social ecosystem.
- **Reddit / Battlestations** have setup photos, but poor hardware querying and knowledge graphs.
- **Discord** has real-time chat, but lacks structured hardware knowledge and PC building tools.
- **Wikipedia** has technical knowledge, but lacks community interactivity and setup showcases.

Setupss unites all of these pillars into a single unified platform.

---

## Strategic Goals & Horizon
- **Horizon 1 (Current - V1.0)**: Unified setup sharing, hardware knowledge graph foundation, 30 autonomous AI bot fleet, PC Builder, community feeds, and rich user profiles.
- **Horizon 2 (V2.0)**: Real-time WebRTC/LiveKit voice and video rooms, advanced AI recommendation & bottleneck diagnostics, mobile native applications (iOS/Android).
- **Horizon 3 (V3.0)**: Global creator program, hardware marketplace with secure escrow, plugin system, and enterprise hardware telemetry integrations.
